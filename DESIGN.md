## Identity

A calm tool that turns a video into one document for an AI. It should leave the impression of a clear, trustworthy step-by-step helper.

## Audience

The owner and close contacts, after a meeting or a class. Desktop and laptop browsers first, phones supported from 320 px wide.

## Personality

It should feel sober and clear, friendly and modern. It must not feel cluttered, flashy or cold.

## Palette

Keep what the page already uses. Accent teal `#0F5C6E` in the light theme and `#5DB8CB` in the dark theme. Light and dark themes both follow the system setting. Teal gives a calm focus color. Neutral grays keep the surfaces quiet. Contrast of both themes must meet WCAG 2.2 AA and is still to be measured.

## Typography

Keep IBM Plex Sans, one family, for readable Spanish text at small sizes.

## Motion

Subtle, matching `motion = subtle` in `.standard.yml`. Only feedback and progress move, for 150 to 300 ms, with transform and opacity. Reduced motion is respected.
